# Product

Inquest is a coroner's inquest for failed or suspicious smart-contract transactions.

The user opens a case from a recorded trace, hears witnesses that may only speak to the record, reads a charge sheet of invariants, and receives a one-page sealed verdict.

Audience: protocol engineers, auditors, and anyone who has to explain an on-chain failure in plain language.

The product is a reader and a checker, not a wallet, not an explorer, and not an exploit tool. Never invent facts. Never handle seed phrases or private keys. Public materials may say the author is in India, and must not include a home address or a personal mobile number.
